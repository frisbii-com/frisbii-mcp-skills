## Week by week

### Week 1 · 26 Oct 2026 · Launch: close October in minutes

* **Moment:** October month end (30 Oct).
* **Hook:** "Your month-end close, done by an assistant before your coffee cools."
* **Primary:** month-end-close (Billing). **Alternate:** payout-reconciliation (Pay).
* **Drives adoption of:** connecting the Frisbii MCP server and installing the pack; a habit that repeats every month end.
* **Channels:** email (segmented by product), in-app/dashboard banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page; launch announcement and CSM outreach.
* **Skill ready by:** 22 Oct 2026. **KPI:** pack installs; MCP connections.
* **Fallback:** if the pack can't be installed by 26 Oct, start on 2 Nov and fold month-end-close into week 6.

### Week 2 · 2 Nov 2026 · Know your customer

* **Hook:** "Walk into every call knowing everything."
* **Primary:** customer-360 (Both).
* **Drives adoption of:** daily use outside finance: CS, sales and account managers checking a customer before a call.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page; CS and sales enablement.
* **Skill ready by:** 29 Oct 2026. **KPI:** installs; tool calls in 7 days.

### Week 3 · 9 Nov 2026 · Fix expiring cards before the peak

* **Moment:** Black Friday prep #1.
* **Hook:** "Fix expiring cards before your biggest weekend, not after."
* **Primary:** card-expiry-sweep (Pay). **Alternate:** account-hygiene (Billing).
* **Drives adoption of:** preventive use, acting on MCP data before payments fail or the busy period starts.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page.
* **Skill ready by:** 5 Nov 2026. **KPI:** installs; tool calls in 7 days.

### Week 4 · 16 Nov 2026 · Campaign readiness check

* **Moment:** Black Friday prep #2.
* **Hook:** "Check your campaign setup before customers find the mistakes."
* **Primary:** promo-readiness (Billing). **Alternate:** payment-method-mix (Pay).
* **Drives adoption of:** catalogue and payment-method checks; brings marketing and ecommerce teams in as users.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page; optional BFCM readiness webinar.
* **Skill ready by:** 12 Nov 2026. **KPI:** installs; tool calls in 7 days.

### Week 5 · 23 Nov 2026 · Watch your peak live

* **Moment:** Black Friday (27 Nov).
* **Hook:** "Watch your peak as it happens."
* **Primary:** peak-pulse (Pay). **Alternate:** peak-pulse sign-up view (Billing).
* **Drives adoption of:** repeated, same-day use during the peak; the highest-volume MCP week of the series.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page; send Mon 23 Nov, reminder Fri 27 Nov.
* **Skill ready by:** 19 Nov 2026. **KPI:** tool calls during the BFCM weekend; error and rate-limit rate.
* **Risk:** the MCP rate limit (~3 calls/min). Consider a temporary limit increase for BFCM week; measure snapshot lag beforehand.

### Week 6 · 30 Nov 2026 · Recover what the rush left behind

* **Moment:** Cyber Monday (30 Nov) and November month end.
* **Hook:** "Recover what the rush left behind."
* **Primary:** failed-payment-triage (Pay). **Alternate:** month-end-close refresher (Billing).
* **Drives adoption of:** diagnosing failed payments with MCP; a second month-end run for Billing merchants.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page.
* **Skill ready by:** 26 Nov 2026. **KPI:** installs; tool calls in 7 days.

### Week 7 · 7 Dec 2026 · Did they stick?

* **Moment:** post-BFCM.
* **Hook:** "Were your Black Friday customers worth the discount?"
* **Primary:** promo-cohort-quality (Billing). **Alternate:** dispute-monitor (Pay).
* **Drives adoption of:** analysis questions answered from snapshot data; growth, risk and finance teams.
* **Channels:** email, in-app banner, LinkedIn clip, skills library page.
* **Skill ready by:** 3 Dec 2026. **KPI:** installs; tool calls in 7 days.
* **Note:** an early read; repeat promo-cohort-quality in February for a fuller picture.

### Week 13 · 18 Jan 2027 · Moving to Frisbii Billing

## Keeping the calendar rolling

* Add new weeks to the "Next weeks" table first; move a week into "At a glance" and "Week by week" once its skill and date are confirmed.
* Keep one primary skill per week, with an alternate for the other product audience where possible.
* Every scheduled skill needs a skill page in this space and a "ready by" date a few days before the episode.
* The source of this page is `docs/marketing-calendar.md` in frisbii-com/frisbii-mcp-skills; edit it there so the page and the repo stay in sync.
